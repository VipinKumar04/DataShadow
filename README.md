# DataShadow AI

**Understand Privacy Policies. Make Informed Decisions.**

DataShadow AI is a project designed to help users understand complex privacy policies in simple language. It aims to identify what information a policy says is collected, how it may be used or shared, how long it may be retained, and what potential privacy concerns users should review.

## 🎯 Project Goal

Our goal is to make privacy policies easier to understand so users can make more informed decisions about their personal data.

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Simple Summaries:** Explain privacy policies in easy-to-understand language.
- **Data Collection Analysis:** Identify categories of information collected and their stated purposes.
- **Third-Party Sharing:** Highlight disclosures about sharing information with other parties.
- **Data Retention:** Explain what the policy says about data retention and deletion.
- **Privacy Concerns:** Highlight potentially concerning clauses for users to review.
- **Evidence-Based Results:** Connect findings to relevant excerpts from the supplied policy text.
- **Recommendations:** Provide practical suggestions for understanding privacy implications.
- **Language Support:** Plan to support English and Hindi.

## 🛠️ Planned Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, EJS
- **Backend:** Node.js and Express.js

## 🔒 Privacy and Limitations

- Analysis will be based on the privacy-policy text provided by the user.
- Findings should include supporting excerpts whenever available.
- Missing information in the supplied text does not prove that a company never performs that practice.
- AI-generated results may contain errors and should be reviewed carefully.
- DataShadow AI is an informational tool, not a substitute for professional legal advice.
